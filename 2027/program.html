Following the traditional N4M rhythm, the meeting begins with a Sunday opening, continues with a full Monday–Wednesday scientific programme, and closes with a Thursday session before departure. Wednesday afternoon remains intentionally flexible for networking, informal discussions, and time to explore the surrounding area.
